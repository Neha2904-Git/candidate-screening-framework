# Candidate Screening Framework 🎯

A structured approach to evaluating candidates consistently against job requirements while maintaining a positive candidate experience.

## 1. Understand the Job Requirement

Before screening candidates, identify:

- Job title and level
- Required technical skills
- Required functional/domain experience
- Years of experience
- Industry experience
- Location and work arrangement
- Notice period / availability
- Compensation range
- Mandatory qualifications

## 2. Resume Screening

Review the candidate's resume for:

| Area | What to Evaluate |
|---|---|
| Experience | Relevance and depth of experience |
| Skills | Match with mandatory job requirements |
| Industry | Relevant industry/domain exposure |
| Career History | Stability and progression |
| Education | Required qualifications |
| Location | Alignment with job location |
| Availability | Notice period and joining timeline |

## 3. Initial Candidate Screening

During the initial conversation, evaluate:

### Technical / Functional Fit
- Relevant experience
- Role-specific knowledge
- Tools and systems used
- Domain expertise

### Communication
- Clarity of communication
- Professionalism
- Ability to explain previous experience

### Motivation
- Reason for considering a change
- Interest in the opportunity
- Career expectations

### Practical Considerations
- Current compensation
- Expected compensation
- Notice period
- Location
- Work arrangement
- Interview availability

## 4. Candidate Evaluation

A simple evaluation model:

| Category | Evaluation |
|---|---|
| Technical / Functional Fit | Strong / Moderate / Low |
| Relevant Experience | Strong / Moderate / Low |
| Communication | Strong / Moderate / Low |
| Industry Experience | Strong / Moderate / Low |
| Compensation Fit | Suitable / Review |
| Notice Period | Suitable / Review |
| Overall Fit | Strong / Moderate / Low |

## 5. Screening Decision

Based on the available information, candidates can be categorized as:

**Shortlist**  
Strong alignment with the essential requirements.

**Further Review**  
Candidate meets some requirements but requires clarification.

**Not Suitable**  
Candidate does not meet essential requirements.

## 6. Interview Coordination

For shortlisted candidates:

1. Confirm candidate availability.
2. Coordinate interview schedules.
3. Share interview details.
4. Follow up with the candidate.
5. Collect interview feedback.
6. Communicate the next steps.

## 7. Candidate Experience

Good candidate experience includes:

- Clear communication
- Timely updates
- Professional interaction
- Accurate information
- Respect for candidate time
- Consistent follow-ups

## 8. Key Principles

A good screening process should be:

- **Structured**
- **Consistent**
- **Job-related**
- **Evidence-based**
- **Candidate-focused**
- **Confidential**

---

### Purpose

This framework is designed as a practical reference for Talent Acquisition professionals involved in candidate sourcing, screening, shortlisting, and recruitment coordination.
